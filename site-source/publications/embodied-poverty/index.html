An Ethnographic and Theoretical Analysis of Embodied Poverty, Spatial Exclusion, and Infrastructural Violence among Shahsavan Nomads

Adel Mohabatian, Asghar Izadi-Jeiran & Nematollah Taghavi

Economic Sociology and Development, 15(2), 113–127

Article pageDownload PDFPublisher pageDOI

Research overview

Drawing on twelve months of ethnographic fieldwork and interviews with 93 participants in the summer and winter pastures of Meshginshahr, this article examines multidimensional poverty among Shahsavan nomads. It identifies bodily exhaustion, uninsured shepherding labor, unsafe migration routes, digital isolation and unreliable drinking water as interconnected manifestations of structural impoverishment shaped by sedentary-centered institutions.
