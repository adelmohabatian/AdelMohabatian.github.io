Research overview

Using library research, this article examines Erving Goffman's influence on contemporary social theory. It considers classical intellectual influences on his work and traces connections with Harold Garfinkel, Anthony Giddens, Jürgen Habermas, Niklas Luhmann, Pierre Bourdieu and the sociology of emotions. It focuses on the lasting role of everyday interaction, social order and self-presentation in later theorization.
