VISUAL ETHNOGRAPHY / TEN PHOTOGRAPHS

Shahsavan: A Life Across the Landscape

A visual field essay by Adel Mohabatian

A field-based visual essay. Five photographs are matched to original files; eight entries retain short provisional captions until their original photographs are recovered. No unrelated images have been substituted.

Beyond the frame

This is an edited selection, not a comprehensive account of Shahsavan life. The contextual captions draw on the field note “Winter-settlement routines and Vahdat’s philosophy of shepherding — 23 Esfand” and related analyses of pastoral skill and work. Image-specific dates, locations, and identities still require verification against the original photo records.
