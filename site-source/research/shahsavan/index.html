RESEARCH DOSSIER / SHAHSAVAN

Shahsavan: pastoral lives and social change

A long-term ethnographic inquiry into the everyday lives of Shahsavan pastoral households in the Meshginshahr region, examining mobility, livelihoods, structural impoverishment and encounters with public institutions.

Research focus

The project draws on ethnographic fieldwork and qualitative inquiry to explore how mobile pastoral livelihoods intersect with infrastructure, bureaucratic procedures, labor relations and changing family arrangements. Its public outputs include journal articles, an ethnographic book in progress, documentary filmmaking and photography.
