About & Academic CV

Profile

Adel Mohabatian is a sociologist and ethnographic researcher whose work brings together long-term fieldwork, qualitative research, documentary filmmaking, and visual storytelling. His research addresses nomadic livelihoods, structural impoverishment, urban informal economies, labor, and social change.

Education

Doctoral research in Sociology (Economic Sociology and Development), University of Tabriz — degree completion status to be confirmed.

M.A. in Sociology (2017); B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering (2009).
