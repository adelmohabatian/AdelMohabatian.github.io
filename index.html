About

05 / Profile

Adel Mohabatian is a sociological researcher, ethnographer and documentary filmmaker based in Tabriz, Iran. His work includes qualitative and ethnographic studies of pastoral livelihoods, urban labor, informal economies and social change. He holds a master's degree in sociology and, according to his 2026 CV, was completing doctoral studies in economic and development sociology. His research and creative practice connect academic inquiry with public-facing visual storytelling.
